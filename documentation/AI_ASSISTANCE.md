The contribution was not limited to language editing. The human author directed the project and ran the reported producer jobs on an Orin system; the results were supplied for analysis.

The delivered archives contain program outputs, not fabricated examples. These checks do not establish global optimization, universal accuracy, or external laboratory replication.

The full private conversation is not included in the public archive. Session-specific model versions and complete prompt histories were not consistently recorded in the experimental archives.
